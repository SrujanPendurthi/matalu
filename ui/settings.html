Matalu Settings

Activation

Hotkey

Changes apply immediately when you save — except switching to or from fn / 🌐 Globe, which takes effect on the next launch.

Cleanup

Removes “um”, stutters, and false starts, and fixes punctuation. Your wording is kept as spoken — nothing is reworded or summarised, and if the cleanup fails the raw text is used. While this is on, text appears when you release the hotkey rather than as you speak, so the whole sentence can be corrected at once.

Meeting transcript

Which microphone to record while transcribing a meeting. To capture the other participants as well as yourself, pick an Aggregate Device that merges your mic with a system-audio loopback (BlackHole). Left on “Default microphone”, a meeting records your side of the call only.
